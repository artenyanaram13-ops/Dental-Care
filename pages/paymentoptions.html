Insurance

Dental Care is in-network with Delta Premier and bills all other providers. Our team is happy to explain how your specific dental benefits apply to the cost of treatment. As a courtesy to our patients, we will collect your estimated co-payment at the time of service, then file a claim to your insurance company on your behalf. If there is any balance remaining, we’ll contact you for final payment.

Not insured? No problem!

We are primarily an out-of-network provider — also known as a “fee-for-service” dentist. Working outside of the insurance network allows us to charge a fair and fixed rate for procedures rather than have fees dictated by a third-party insurance provider. Being a fee-for-service dental office allows us to recommend more conservative treatment options. Our philosophy is that the less dental treatment we can do for you to keep you healthy, the better off you are! Questions about the estimated cost of treatment or paying for your care? Don’t hesitate to reach out.
